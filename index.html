UNDER THE HOOD

How DES works

A block cipher, one transformation at a time.
01

Initial permutation

Each 64-bit plaintext block is rearranged using a fixed permutation table, splitting the block into left and right halves.

+
02

Key generation

The 64-bit key is permuted, split, and shifted to produce sixteen unique 48-bit round keys.

+
03

16 Feistel rounds

Expansion, key mixing, substitution, and permutation transform the right half in every round.

+
04

Final permutation

After the sixteenth round, the two halves are recombined and the inverse permutation forms the ciphertext block.

+
05

Decryption process

DES decryption runs the same Feistel structure with the sixteen generated keys in reverse order.
